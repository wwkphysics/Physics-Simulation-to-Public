About this model. This is an idealised one-dimensional model with uniform initial electron energies from 0 to Kmax, emission towards the collector, a uniform electric field and complete collection at positive voltage. Electrons with positive initial energy are also collected at zero voltage. The straight retarding section illustrates this chosen energy distribution; real experimental I–V curves can have different shapes. Photon arrival rate is held fixed when wavelength changes. The displayed current is a steady model value, not a count of the animated dots. Uses c ≈ 3.00 × 10⁸ m s⁻¹ and hc ≈ 1240 eV nm.

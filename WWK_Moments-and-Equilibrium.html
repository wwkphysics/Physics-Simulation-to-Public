Model assumptions

Moment equals force multiplied by perpendicular distance from the reference point to the line of action. Anticlockwise moments are positive. In Equilibrium mode the beam is free and O is its centre of mass. The calculation uses the initial horizontal position and gives the initial tendency to translate or turn.

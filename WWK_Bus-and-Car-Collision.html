Explain the effects

At each instant the two contact forces have equal magnitudes and opposite directions. They act simultaneously on different objects.

In this model, compression increases with force and decreases with stiffness. Compare the two vehicles with equal stiffness to isolate the effect of mass on acceleration.

Model assumptions

The motion is one-dimensional. Horizontal forces during contact come from the bumpers. The model treats the two contacting ends as elastic springs in series. The animation is slowed down and the time axis shows model time.

Vehicle masses and stiffnesses are illustrative. The diagram shows temporary compression during contact. Real vehicle deformation can be permanent and depends on the structure and impact conditions.

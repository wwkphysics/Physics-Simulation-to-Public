Explain the result

At the same height, both trolleys have fallen through the same vertical distance h. On the smooth tracks, the loss of gravitational potential energy equals the gain in kinetic energy, so both trolleys have the same speed at that height.

The trolleys can pass that height at different times. The comparison view shows each trolley at the moment it passes the selected height.

mgh = ½mv2  →  v = √(2gh)

Each trolley has a mass of 1.0 kg. The model uses g = 9.81 m s⁻², takes the finish as zero gravitational potential energy and ignores wheel rotation and air resistance. Each display freezes the trolley’s readings at the finish.
